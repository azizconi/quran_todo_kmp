Quran Hifz helps you memorize the Quran step by step: a short lesson every day, review on the right day, and what you have learned stays with you.

For anyone on the way to becoming a hafiz, memorizing Juz Amma, studying with a teacher or returning to the Quran.

YOUR LESSON IS READY EVERY DAY
Built on the traditional sabaq, sabqi and manzil:
• New: a short passage for today. Listen, read, then recite it from memory.
• Recent: passages from the last weeks come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
• Old: what you know well goes round in a circle, so nothing is lost.
After each ayah, mark "I remember" or "Hard". Hard ayahs come back until they stick.

A PLAN THAT FITS YOU
Choose Juz Amma, Juz Tabarak, the whole Quran or your own surahs. Set a pace in ayahs a day or a finish date, and see when you will complete it. Surahs you already know go straight into review.

TEST YOURSELF
• Recall with hints: first letters, every other word or gradual hiding.
• Quizzes: continue the ayah, name the next one, put ayahs in order, find where an ayah is.
• Tell similar ayahs apart and practise your hard places.

READ AND LISTEN
• Mushaf pages like the printed Quran (604 pages, 15 lines), or clear text in a large font.
• 12 reciters, including Mishary Alafasy, Sudais, Shuraim, Husary and Abdul Basit, plus a slow Husary recitation for memorizing.
• Each word lights up as it is recited. Tap a word to start from it.
• Repeat a passage, or use echo mode: the reciter reads, then it is your turn. Audio keeps playing with the screen off.
• Colour-coded tajweed with a legend.

UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU MEMORIZE
• Several translations for each language and tafsir for every ayah.
• Word-by-word meanings (in English).
• Similar ayahs with the differences highlighted.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Day streak, a map of the whole Quran, progress by juz and a forecast for your plan. See how well you remember each surah and where it is hardest. Share a report with your teacher as an image, PDF or text.

AND MORE
• A reminder at the time you choose. If today's lesson is done, it stays silent. Pause the plan for 1–14 days and keep your streak.
• Khatm plan for 30 days or by any date. In Ramadan: a one-month khatm in one tap and "Juz of the night" for taraweeh.
• Home Screen widget with today's lesson and your streak.
• Works offline: the Arabic text is built in, and audio and translations can be downloaded ahead.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No account and no analytics. Your progress is stored on your iPhone, and you can move it to another phone with a file.

Languages: English, Russian, Turkish, Tajik and Uzbek.

Quran Hifz does not check your recitation and does not replace a teacher: you judge yourself, and the app keeps the schedule.
