2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Quran Hifz helps Muslims memorize the Quran by heart (hifz) and keep what they have memorized. The common problem is forgetting: people learn new passages but do not know what to review and when. Each day the app prepares one lesson in the traditional order used by Quran teachers: new passage (sabaq), recent passages (sabqi, repeated after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days) and old passages (manzil, in a circle). The audience is students memorizing the Quran (children with parents, adults, students of a teacher), people memorizing Juz Amma, and anyone returning to the Quran.

3. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES (no login, no setup files needed)
• Launch the app and complete the short onboarding: choose the language, what to learn (for example Juz Amma) and the pace (ayahs per day). Optionally mark surahs you already know.
• Lesson tab: tap the lesson card to start today's lesson. Listen to the ayah, read it, recall it from memory and answer "I remember" or "Hard".
• Quran tab: pick any surah to read it as text or as mushaf pages; press play to listen to a reciter, the current word is highlighted; long-press or tap an ayah for translation and tafsir.
• Check (from the Lesson tab): recall with hints and quizzes on memorized ayahs.
• Progress tab: streak, map of the Quran, progress by juz, and a report that can be shared as an image, PDF or text.
• Settings: reminder time, reciter, translation, downloads for offline use, and saving the progress to a file to move it to another phone.
An internet connection is needed only the first time audio or a translation is opened; the Arabic text works offline.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
The app has no own server, no authentication, no payments, no ads, no analytics and no AI services. It downloads public Quran content on demand from:
• api.alquran.cloud and api.islamic.network (translations, tafsir, timings)
• api.quran.com and quran.com resources (translations, word meanings, tajweed markup, mushaf page layout)
• quranicaudio.com and related public audio hosts (recitation audio)
• quranenc.com (translations)
The Arabic Quran text (Tanzil) and fonts (SIL Open Font License) are bundled. The full list of sources with links and licenses is shown in the app under Settings > Sources.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is available in English, Russian, Turkish, Tajik and Uzbek and follows the language chosen in the app.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The Quran text, translations, tafsir and recitations are published by their sources for free public use and are credited in Settings > Sources, as the sources request. The app does not operate in a regulated industry.
