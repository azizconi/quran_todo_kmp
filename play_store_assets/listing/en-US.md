## Title
Quran Hifz: Memorize Quran

## Short description
A ready Quran memorization lesson every day, with reviews on the right day.

## Full description
Memorize the Quran every day — a little at a time, and never forget it.

Quran Hifz gives you one clear lesson a day and brings back what you already know before you forget it. It is built for people who learn on their own, with a teacher, or are simply returning to the Quran.

LESSON OF THE DAY
Open the app and the lesson is ready, in the traditional order:
• New — a short passage to learn today. Listen, read, then recall it from memory.
• Recent — passages from the last days and weeks, repeated on a ladder: +1, +3, +7, +14 and +30 days.
• Old — the passages you know well, passed in a circle so that nothing is lost.
After each ayah you answer honestly: "I remember" or "Hard". Hard ayahs are marked and come back until they stick.

MY PLAN AND FORECAST
Choose what to learn — Juz Amma, Juz Tabarak, the Quran from the start or your own surahs — and set the pace in ayahs a day, or pick a date you want to finish by. The app shows when you will finish. Already know some surahs? Mark them: they go straight into the review circle instead of being taught again.

A REMINDER THAT RESPECTS YOU
Pick the time yourself. If today's lesson is already done, the reminder does not arrive.

READ AND LISTEN
• The text of the mushaf with Juz, Hizb and page marks, a translation, a large font and light, dark or system theme.
• Four reciters: Mishary Alafasy, Husary, Minshawi and Abdul Basit.
• Repeat a passage as many times as you need; playback continues with the screen off.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
The Arabic text is inside the app. Audio and translations can be downloaded ahead of time in "Downloads and storage" (with a Wi-Fi only option).

HONEST PROGRESS
Day streak, activity for the last 30 days, a map of the whole Quran and progress by juz. One tap prepares a report for your teacher (ustaz): what you learned, what you reviewed and which ayahs are hard.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No account, no sign-up, no ads.
• Anonymous statistics and crash reports help us find bugs; you can switch them off in the app.
• Move your progress to another phone through a file. There is no cloud sync.

LANGUAGES
Russian, English, Tajik, Uzbek and Turkish.

GOOD TO KNOW
Quran Hifz does not listen to your recitation and does not replace a teacher: you judge yourself, the app keeps the schedule.
