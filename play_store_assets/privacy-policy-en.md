# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Quran Hifz** (package `tj.app.quran_todo`, "the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Android application (the iOS app is described in section 10). The short version: there are no accounts, your study data stays on your phone, and the only data that leaves the device is what is needed to download the Quran content (text, translations, tafsir, mushaf pages, audio) and, unless you turn it off, anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Data stored on your device only

Your learning progress, ayah and surah statuses, notes, goals, reminders, reading plan (khatm), similar-ayah index, quiz results, detailed statistics and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not have a server that receives them and we cannot see them. The App has no accounts: you do not sign in, and nothing is linked to your name, e-mail address or phone number.

### 1.2 Microphone

The App does not use the microphone and does not record sound. It does not ask for the microphone permission.

### 1.3 Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

The App uses **Google Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics**. They are **on by default** and can be turned off at any time in **Settings > Privacy > Anonymous statistics and crash reports**. While the switch is off, no statistics or crash reports are sent, and the data the analytics SDK has stored on the device is cleared when you switch it off.

When on, the following is collected:

- usage events (for example which screens are opened and which features are used, with technical values such as a surah number or the app language, but never the text of your notes or searches);
- device information: device model, manufacturer, Android version, screen and language settings, app version;
- crash reports and non-fatal error reports (technical stack traces and the state of the app at the time);
- an app installation identifier generated by Firebase for your installation of the App (it is not your name, e-mail address or the Android advertising ID);
- an approximate location (country or region) that Google derives from your IP address.

The App **does not use the Android advertising ID** (the `AD_ID` permission is removed), does not show ads and does not use the data for advertising or ad personalization. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase terms; see https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

### 1.4 Network requests to content providers

To download the Quran text, translations, surah names and recitation audio, the App contacts the following servers:

- `api.alquran.cloud` (translations and the tajweed markup of the text, that is which reading rules apply to which letters; Islamic Network; the Quran text itself is bundled with the app);
- `quranenc.com` (translations and the "Al-Mukhtasar" tafsir, QuranEnc);
- `api.quran.com` (surah names, translations, the line layout of the mushaf pages, tafsir, word meanings in English and the word timings of recitations, Quran.com);
- `verses.quran.com` (recitation audio and the fonts of the mushaf pages, Quran.com);
- `cdn.islamic.network` (recitation audio, Islamic Network);
- `mirrors.quranicaudio.com` (recitation audio, QuranicAudio).

These servers receive the technical data every internet request contains, in particular your **IP address**, and a description of what is requested (for example a surah, a mushaf page, a translation, an audio file or a font). We do not send them your progress, notes, settings or any other study data. What you download is kept on your phone so that it works offline.

### 1.5 Widget, khatm, similar ayahs and notes

The "Lesson of the day" widget shows the lesson on your home screen. Its data comes from the database on your phone and **does not leave the device**. The reading plan (khatm), the similar-ayah index (it is bundled with the app), quizzes and "My notes" also work only on the device and send nothing to any server. Weekly or monthly reports (picture, PDF, text) and the transfer file are created on your phone; where they go is your decision, when you pick an app to send them to or a place to save them.

### 1.6 Backup by Android

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android (Google Backup) may store a copy of the App's database and settings in **your Google account** and restore it when you reinstall the App or set up a new phone, or transfer it directly to a new phone. This is a feature of your device; we do not receive that copy. Downloaded audio, caches and your own recordings are not backed up. You can turn the backup off in your device settings (Settings > Google > Backup).

## 2. Permissions

- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: to download Quran content, translations, tafsir, mushaf pages and audio, and to send the anonymous statistics described above.
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: used only if you enable reminders; Android asks you before the App can show notifications.
- `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`: so that the lesson reminder is scheduled again after your phone restarts.
- `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` and `WAKE_LOCK`: so that recitation keeps playing with the screen off.
- `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC`: so that a download of audio and translations in "Downloads and storage" can finish while the app is in the background.
- `BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE` (`com.google.android.finsky.permission`): added by the Firebase library; through it Google Play tells the library where the App was installed from. We do not use it for advertising.
- The App does not access your contacts, location, camera or photos. It accesses a file only when you yourself pick a transfer file or a place to save a report.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

- provide the App's features (reading, listening, memorization, review, self-checks, progress, the widget and reminders);
- download Quran content and related resources (text, translations, tafsir, mushaf pages and their fonts, audio);
- find and fix bugs and understand which features are used, so that we can improve the App (anonymous statistics and crash reports, if you leave them on).

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes. Information reaches only the service providers named above, and only as described: Google (Firebase) for statistics and crash reports, and the content providers (Islamic Network, Quran.com, QuranEnc, QuranicAudio) for downloading Quran content.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App has no accounts, no ads and no social features, and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Students under 13 may use the App with the guidance of a parent, guardian or teacher where required. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will review and remove it where applicable.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

- Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the App or clear its data (Android Settings > Apps > Quran Hifz > Storage > Clear data).
- A copy in Android backup is managed in your Google account and your device settings.
- Anonymous statistics and crash reports are kept by Google for a limited period set in Firebase. Because they are not linked to a name or an account, we cannot look up or delete the data of a particular person; you can stop further collection by turning the switch in Settings off, or by uninstalling the App.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, and connections to content providers use HTTPS. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the App gets new features. Updated versions will be published with a revised effective date, and the in-app privacy summary will be updated with the App.

## 10. The iOS app

The iOS version of Quran Hifz **collects no analytics and no crash data**. It does not include Firebase or any other analytics or crash-reporting library, and it has no accounts. Your progress, notes and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad (and in your iCloud backup, if you have turned iCloud backup on in Apple's settings; we cannot see it). The only data that leaves the device is the requests that download Quran audio, translations and tajweed markup from the public sources listed in section 1.4; those servers can see your IP address and what you asked for, as with any download. The iOS app does not ask for the microphone or for access to your contacts, location or photos, except to save a report image to Photos if you choose to. Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device. The anonymous statistics switch described in section 1.3 exists only in the Android app.
