Frequently asked questions

How is the daily lesson built?

Each lesson has three parts: New (a short passage for today), Recent (passages from the last weeks, repeated after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days) and Old (what you know well, in a circle). After each ayah you mark "I remember" or "Hard"; hard ayahs come back sooner.

I already know some surahs. Do I have to learn them again?

No. Mark them as known in your plan and they go straight into review.

Does the app work without the internet?

Yes. The Arabic text is built into the app. Recitation audio and translations are downloaded when you first open them, or ahead of time in "Downloads and storage".

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Open Settings and save a copy of your progress to a file, then open that file in Quran Hifz on the new phone. There is no account and no cloud sync, so keep the file somewhere safe.

Does the app check my recitation?

No. Quran Hifz does not listen to you and does not replace a teacher: you judge yourself, and the app keeps the schedule.

What data do you collect?

The iOS app collects no data: no account, no analytics, no crash reports. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
